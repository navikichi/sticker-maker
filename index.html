How to use your stickers

  • iPhone (iOS 17 or newer), for all apps: pick File type: PNG and save. Open the picture in Photos, press and hold the character, then tap Add Sticker. It is now in your sticker drawer: tap the 😊 key on the keyboard, then the sticker icon.
  • Messenger on Android: pick File type: GIF, then tap Share and pick Messenger. Stickers come out small and see-through.
  • Facebook, Viber, Instagram: tap Share and pick the app, or send the saved picture like a photo.
  • WhatsApp: pick File type: PNG, save all, then add them with a free app like “Sticker Maker” (it needs at least 3 stickers).
  • Telegram: pick File type: PNG, then send the saved pictures to @Stickers to make your own pack.
